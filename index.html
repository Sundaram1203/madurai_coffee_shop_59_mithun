About Us

Serving SINCE 2016

Our Story

Born in the heart of Madurai, our coffee shop began as a small family-run space with a deep passion for authentic South Indian filter coffee.

What started as a humble stall has grown into a cozy destination where tradition meets taste. We proudly serve freshly brewed degree coffee made from handpicked beans sourced from the Western Ghats, ensuring rich aroma and perfect flavor in every cup. Each sip reflects our heritage and the vibrant culture of Madurai. From early morning regulars to relaxed evening gatherings, our journey has always been about bringing people together over great coffee.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a symbol of authentic Madurai coffee culture, preserving tradition while embracing modern flavors.

Deliver rich and authentic filter coffee experience
